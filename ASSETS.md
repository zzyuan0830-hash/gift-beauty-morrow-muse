# Asset provenance

`public/art/paper-study.svg` and `public/art/ribbon-study.svg` are original vector illustrations created for this implementation. They are decorative paper/ribbon studies, have descriptive alternative text and do not depict saleable products. No Bingkui image, copy or commercial data was used. No remote artwork or v2 AI concept merchandise is copied into the commercial catalog.

Real product images are delivered from Shopify CDN only after product image verification. The merchant must supply actual product photos and record usage rights in `docs/PRODUCT-INTAKE.csv`. At least three distinct image paths and nonempty alt text are enforced, but uniqueness of camera angle and product correspondence require human review.

Browser test images are generated SVG fixtures intercepted only by Playwright. They explicitly say TEST FIXTURE / NOT A REAL PRODUCT and are not in `public/`. Fixture screenshots are test evidence, not commercial photography or a representation of finalized merchandise.

Fonts: system Arial/Helvetica and Georgia/Times New Roman; no downloaded fonts, font services or paid assets. Runtime libraries retain their upstream licenses in dependencies; the standalone build includes the necessary third-party notices. No trademark or domain clearance has been performed for the working name Morrow & Muse.

`app/icon.svg` is an original M monogram used as the site icon.
